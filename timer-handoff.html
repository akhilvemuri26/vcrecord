Automatic preparation and recording

The practice flow now reveals each question together with a 15-second preparation countdown, then automatically records camera video and microphone audio for 20 seconds and saves. Preparation contributes no recorded video, PCM, or saved duration. After saving, the current question remains visible; Next question starts the next automatic cycle and Practice again repeats it.

Entry and settings

Initial page load, including returning users, requires one Start practice gesture to acquire browser media. Next requires no separate record action. After Answers or cancellation, the user explicitly starts practice again. The question stays hidden through permission, preview playback, worklet loading, and AudioContext readiness. Timing settings persist per browser: preparation 1–120 whole seconds; answers 5–30 whole seconds, within the existing transcription window. Invalid settings have visible feedback; corrupt stored values use 15/20 defaults.

Lifecycle safeguards

Attempt generations, AbortSignal cancellation, and single-shot capture completion prevent stale timers and late permission grants from restoring capture or saving duplicates. Cancel, Next, Answers, settings, hidden tabs, pagehide, and ended tracks cancel setup/preparation and release resources. Recording retains Stop & save and hidden-page save behavior. Identity, question, team attribution, and timing are frozen for each attempt. Saving/rescued answers block replacement capture. Transcription progress cannot overwrite active timer status. Shared Supabase, local Whisper, durable synchronization, and transcript draft safeguards remain in place.
