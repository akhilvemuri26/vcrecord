October 14, 2026 · /Users/akhilvemuri/coding/vcrecord

Implemented the approved plan. “Test mic & video” opens an immediate five-second sample with live camera preview and a meter derived from recognition PCM. It then releases camera/microphone resources and offers a video player with sound available by user gesture. Samples remain in memory; closing or retesting discards them. No name or team is required.

Limits and review notes

All browser media evidence is synthetic, not real hardware verification. The user’s original browser/device and whether answer playback contains their voice remain unknown. The original failure’s cause is unconfirmed; removing the threshold does not establish that it caused that particular failure. The diagnostics identify PCM capture/input state, not speech intelligibility. Exact zero is the only nonempty input skipped before recognition; low nonzero input may still yield no transcript.

The existing full timer-browser-check.mjs was not rerun; default 15/20 timing and practice PCM/video gating were covered by the existing Node tests, while normal capture after the test was verified in Chromium using bounded custom timing.
