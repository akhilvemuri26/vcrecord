Implemented behavior

  • Answers immediately reads every nondeleted local Blob regardless of team or browser identity. Remote answers and pending overlays are projected only from confirmed session memberships. Deduplication preserves local durable content; owner/team-matching tombstones suppress fetched records.
  • Startup restores authentication and discovers accessible teams even without a saved team preference. New shared recordings use the confirmed canonical destination; recording during connection recovery stays local. Ordinary unmapped memberships retain an accessible deterministic fallback.
  • Invitation references keep their original team ID and token separately from the new-recording destination. Both legacy and current private invites use existing join validation; no secret is bundled. The Create shared team action is removed. Copy invite requires an actually held original pair and confirmed accessibility.
  • Person dropdowns cover Akhil, Prakrit, Aarav; Answers also offers historical labels and Unassigned when present. Whitespace/case are normalized, and Akhil Vemuri maps to Akhil only for display/filtering. Stored person and owner remain unchanged.
  • Most recent and By question views have visible/total counts, selection retention with newest fallback, person-specific empty states, clear local/shared and exceptional statuses. Connected status is never written over membership, partial fetch, or upload failures.
  • Identity changes clear remote caches immediately. Refresh generations and session checks reject stale results. A failed refresh can retain same-session saved results with an honest warning. Rescue recording, capture/transcription behavior, media test and timing defaults remain intact.
