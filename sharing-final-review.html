Final sharing correction review: approved

14 October 2026 · Fresh independent Astra rereview. The P1 cross-tab deleted-recording resurrection reported in the original review is corrected. No introduced blocking defect was found in this focused review. The correction is approved for publication.

Why the correction resolves the finding

src/sync.ts now acquires the same origin-wide exclusive Web Lock, vcrecord:shared-answer-sync, before entering the entire durable-record read, remote write and acknowledgment loop. A competing tab cannot remove the remote recording and local tombstone while an earlier tab still uploads that recording. Once the upload completes, the next durable read sees the deletion and removes both remote metadata and its video object before removing the tombstone.

Queued drains read durable state after acquiring the lock. Revision-aware acknowledgments preserve newer edits; subsequent iterations send their current content. Existing per-tab request handling continues to coalesce triggers while waiting. Missing or rejected Web Locks prevent sharing network writes, retain local video and pending deletion state, and notify the interface of the synchronization error.
