Behavior

The original capture deadline and question provenance remain. Team members type their name and record; new videos and transcripts upload automatically after local storage commits. Team invitations use a 32-byte random secret in a URL fragment, cleared before asynchronous work, including same-tab fragment navigation. Authentication uses a persisted anonymous session. The private bucket and database enforce team membership for reads and the authenticated recording owner for edits/deletions; display names only control grouping. People with the same trimmed case-normalized name group together across browsers.

Files and backend

src/sharing.ts contains the public browser client and metadata/video APIs. src/sync.ts serializes durable revision-aware writes and deletions. src/storage.ts preserves the existing IndexedDB v1 store and source Blob/audio, adds synchronization state, and rejects late edits to tombstones. src/main.ts merges remote records with local changes, provides owner controls, and guards asynchronous refresh/playback. supabase/migrations/20261014000000_sharing.sql reproduces the team/RPC/RLS/private-storage setup.

Durability and limits

Local saves are retained after successful sharing. Network failure retains pending revisions and playable source video. Video uploads are create-only; retries verify existing private objects. Transcript changes upload separately after video succeeds. Successful sends mark only matching revisions synchronized. Deletions retain tombstones until remote metadata and object deletion succeed; late Whisper messages cannot recreate records. Remote-owned edits and deletions also persist as local pending metadata.

Anonymous ownership belongs to a browser session. Clearing site/auth data loses that editing identity; reopening an invitation restores team reading and new recordings but cannot recover editing rights on old answers. Transcription still runs locally, and its prior real Whisper evidence remains applicable because model/capture implementation was preserved. No Realtime or SSR framework was introduced.

Publication and final HTTPS smoke check remain root-owned. Independent review found cross-tab drain serialization needed an additional correction; a fresh correction manager owns src/sync.ts and its regression tests, followed by fresh review. Task servers were stopped before handoff.
